PayCheck Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

In short: PayCheck does not collect, send, or share any of your information. Your budget lives only on your phone, and nobody — including the developer — can see it.

What PayCheck stores

PayCheck saves the things you enter — your pay schedules, income, bills, groups, savings goals, and settings — in a database in the app's private storage on your device. That is the only place it is kept.

What PayCheck collects

Nothing. PayCheck:

  • does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere;
  • has no accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync;
  • contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting libraries;
  • requests no Android permissions for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Android backup is turned off

PayCheck opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your budget is not copied to your Google account or to another phone without your action.

Backup files you create

Settings has Save a backup and Send a backup somewhere. PayCheck only creates a backup file when you tap one of these, and you choose where it goes through Android's own save or share screen. Once you save or send it — for example, to a cloud drive or an email — that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

When you use Restore from a backup, PayCheck reads the file you pick on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Buying PayCheck

PayCheck is a paid app, and the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer never receives your card or other payment details — only the sales records Google provides for refunds, accounting, and taxes, such as the order date, price, and country.

Google Play may also give the developer aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash rates. None of this ever includes the contents of your budget.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data permanently erases your budget data from your phone. Uninstalling PayCheck, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, does the same. Backup files you saved or sent stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

Children

PayCheck is a personal-finance tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
